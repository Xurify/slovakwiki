# Entry rail removal

## Decision

Remove the left rail from individual reference entries. It repeats classification already shown in the breadcrumb and metadata, and its browse links do not filter or navigate to distinct destinations.

## Result

Desktop entries use two columns: a readable article and a useful right context rail. The right rail keeps page anchors and related entries. At narrower widths, it moves below the article.

## Boundaries

The `/wiki` index sidebar stays. Its filters change visible content, so it has a clear purpose.
