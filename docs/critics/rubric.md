# Episode rubric (v3.2, shaped by Xurify's reviews of episode 5, its acted kitchen, the robot stories and the listener's game)

For any listening episode, a show or a story. v1 lived per thread; v2 merged them and added what the review of #76 asked for (the old text is `episode-rubric-v2.1.md`). v3 takes the scoring away from the writer and ties each dimension to the failure classes A–H in section 6 of `docs/listening-one-prompt.md`. Each dimension is out of 10. Add each new lesson with the review it came from, as a class rule and a critic question, not only a line here. v3.1 adds dimension 10, motion and contact, after the kitchen's frame-by-frame review (v3 is `episode-rubric-v3.md`). v3.2 adds dimension 11, the player and the listener's game, and the calque and whole-phrase rules (D6, D7), after Xurify's play-through of the game in #80 (v3.1 is `episode-rubric-v3.1.md`).

**Who scores.** Never the writer: drafts the writer scored 9/10 had a dozen problems each. The score comes from the critics' last round (`docs/critics/script-critic.md` run twice and merged, `docs/critics/picture-critic.md` on the contact sheets, and `docs/critics/motion-critic.md` on strips of every acted segment) and from CI:

- Start at 10. Take off 2 for each open finding a first-time viewer would notice, and 1 for each nit.
- A CI error in the dimension's rules caps it at 5: CI fails anyway, so fix it first.
- A finding the writer rejected counts as open unless the council (`docs/critics/council.md`) upheld the rejection. List the council's verdicts in the PR.

**Anchors for every dimension.** 10: nothing a careful reviewer would change. 9: one small nit. 7–8: issues a listener would notice. 5–6: a reviewer would ask for a rewrite of that part. Below 5: broken.

**Where to judge the picture.** On the episode page (`/listening/<slug>`), played through at 390 px and desktop, light and dark. Never `/dev/scenes`: it is a different player.
